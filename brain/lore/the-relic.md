# The Relic

The story's time-travel mechanism and central magic-system artifact.
Explicitly original mythology — not modeled on any existing
power-passing system (e.g. Green Lantern, Ms. Marvel).

## What it is

An ancient, adaptive artifact passed keeper to keeper — not by
bloodline, but chosen for fit. Its abilities adapt to whatever each
era/story needs rather than being a fixed toolkit. Only one active
keeper at a time.

## Keeper lineage (as known so far)

1. Nova's mother — an unaware keeper. Never knew what she carried or
   used it. Chosen for fit, not blood.
2. Nova (Samantha Novak) — current keeper as of *Birth of a Hero*
   through *The Defender Chronicles*. Fully aware of what she carries,
   unlike her mother.
3. Samantha Reyes — receives it from Nova in a real playable scene
   opening *Legacy: New Frontier*, becoming the next (and, per "only
   one keeper at a time," sole) active keeper.

## Mechanics (as they apply to gameplay)

- **Range:** full — any past issue, any era, no distance limit.
- **Regeneration:** progress-based, gradual buildup over roughly a
  couple of levels' worth of play. Not real-time, not instant.
- **Availability:** from the very first level of the game (*Birth of a
  Hero*, Series I) — not a mid-story unlock. An earlier draft had
  considered a mid-story (2060 era) unlock; that was dropped.
- **After a rewind + replay:** the player chooses **Snap Forward** (a
  short recap cutscene, drops back to where they left off on the new
  branch) or **Play It Out** (continues live through everything after,
  on the new branch).

## Not yet known / not yet loaded

Exactly what "adapts to what each era needs" means in concrete ability
terms for either keeper, and how Samantha Reyes's version of the Relic
differs in practice from her mother's.
