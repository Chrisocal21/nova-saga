## Resolved (kept for reference)

- **Platform:** browser/PWA, linked from the ProbablyFineStudios site.
- **Art direction:** painted/inked characters, realistic environments,
  moody muted palette with amber/rust dusk-lighting accents.
- **Monetization:** free for now, optional pre-story video later, never
  interrupting the narrative.
- **Time travel range and regeneration rules:** see `lore/the-relic.md`.
- **The "Issue 58 / Rebirth: A New Dream" continuity flag:** near-certain
  match to "Rebirth: A New Dawn," the real *Legacy of a Hero* Series II
  title.
- **Reading format:** single full-screen panel replacing the last, not
  a continuous scroll (revised after prototyping — see
  `markdown files/NOVA_PROJECT_LOG.md`).
