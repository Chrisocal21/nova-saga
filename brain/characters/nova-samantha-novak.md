# Nova (Samantha Novak)

Playable protagonist for the first two trilogies: *Nova: Birth of a
Hero* and *Nova: The Defender Chronicles*.

- **Hero name:** Nova
- **Civilian name:** Samantha Novak
- **Hair color:** unconfirmed — inconsistent across existing reference
  sheets (blonde vs. brown). See `continuity/open-threads.md`. The
  placeholder art/script for the Feature 1.1 engine prototype defaulted
  her to brown hair; this is not a canon decision.
- **The Relic:** given to her by her mother, who was chosen as keeper
  for fit rather than blood and never knew what she carried or used it.
  Nova is the sole active keeper until she hands it off. See
  `lore/the-relic.md`.
- **Hand-off:** passes the Relic to Samantha Reyes in a real playable
  scene at the start of *Legacy of a Hero*, Series I. Unlike her mother,
  Nova loses the ability the moment she hands it off — she knows
  exactly what she's giving up.
- **"Shocking secret about Nova's past":** referenced in both *Birth of
  a Hero* Series II and *Legacy of a Hero* Series II. Not yet
  determined whether this is the same event told twice or two separate
  reveals — see `continuity/open-threads.md`.
