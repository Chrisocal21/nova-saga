# Samantha Reyes (Sam / Nova)

Playable protagonist for the third trilogy, *Nova: Legacy of a Hero*.

- **Given name:** Samantha Reyes — named after her mother, Samantha
  Novak (Nova)
- **Goes by:** Sam day-to-day; later nicknamed "Nova" by accident
  (presumably by others drawing the connection to her mother — the
  exact circumstance of the nickname sticking isn't detailed yet)
- **Parents:** Nova (Samantha Novak) and Daniel Reyes
- **The Relic:** receives it from her mother in a real playable scene
  that opens *Legacy: New Frontier*, as she leaves for her first
  journey. She becomes the sole active keeper at that point. See
  `lore/the-relic.md`.
- **"Shocking secret about Nova's past":** also referenced in this
  trilogy's Series II — see the open question on `nova-samantha-novak.md`
  and `continuity/open-threads.md` about whether this is the same
  reveal as Birth of a Hero's or a distinct one.
